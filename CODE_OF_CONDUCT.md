# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. This is a small project about making a build dashboard less annoying; nothing here is
worth being unpleasant to a stranger over.

## What that means

**Expected:** patience with people who know less than you, precision over point-scoring, and
accepting that a maintainer may say no to your idea and still be glad you raised it.

**Not accepted:** harassment of any kind, insults, demeaning comments, sustained disruption,
publishing others' private information, or unwelcome attention — public or private.

This applies to issues, pull requests, commit messages, code review, and anywhere else the project
is being discussed.

## If something goes wrong

Report it to **karangandhi486@gmail.com**. Reports are handled privately. The maintainer may edit
or remove comments, close threads, or block accounts, and does not owe a public explanation for
doing so where the alternative is dragging someone through a thread.
